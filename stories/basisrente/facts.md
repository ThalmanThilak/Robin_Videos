# Facts used in the film (each with a source)

| Claim | Source | Checked |
|---|---|---|
| Basisrente contributions count as pension expenses (Sonderausgaben). Since 2023 they are 100% deductible up to the limit. | Verbraucherzentrale Hamburg: https://www.vzhh.de/print/pdf/node/3740 | 01 Nov 2026 |
| Limit 2026: EUR 30,826 single, EUR 61,652 married. Derived from 124,800 x 24.7% (knappschaftliche RV). | MLP: https://mlp.de/finanzen-versicherungen/altersvorsorge/basisrente/ and steuerschroeder.de: https://www.steuerschroeder.de/Steuerrechner/Ruerup-Rente.html (both not official, but they match the rule in EStG section 10 (3)) | 01 Nov 2026 |
| Employee and employer state pension contributions use up part of that same limit. | ruv.de: https://www.ruv.de/altersvorsorge/basisrente-sonderausgabenabzug | 01 Nov 2026 |
| BBG 2026: EUR 101,400 general RV, EUR 124,800 knappschaftliche RV. Rates 18.6% and 24.7%. | krankenkassen.de: https://www.krankenkassen.de/gesetzliche-krankenkassen/system-gesetzliche-krankenversicherung/sozialversicherung-rechengroessen-beitragsbemessungsgrenze-versicherungspflichtgrenze/rechengroessen-2026/ | 01 Nov 2026 |
| Pension is paid monthly for life. It is taxed in retirement. | Verbraucherzentrale Hamburg (above) | 01 Nov 2026 |

## The example (labelled "Example" on screen, ASSUMED numbers)
- Jonas earns about EUR 90,000 a year. State pension contributions (employee + employer): 18.6% x 90,000 = EUR 16,740.
- Room left in the 2026 limit: 30,826 - 16,740 = EUR 14,086. EUR 3,600 a year fits easily.
- EUR 300 x 12 = EUR 3,600. At an assumed 42% marginal tax rate: 3,600 x 0.42 = EUR 1,512, shown as "about EUR 1,500".
- Not counted: Soli, church tax. The 42% rate is an assumption, not checked against the 2026 tax table.

Rule: the end card says "Example only. Tax depends on your income. The pension is taxed in retirement."
